About the Department of Computer Science

Striving for academic excellence, innovative research, and ethical tech leadership

🏢 Department Overview

Established with a vision to develop technically competent and ethically grounded professionals, the Department of Computer Science has grown into a leading hub of academic and research excellence. We offer rigorous undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs that blend foundational principles with high-demand practical technologies.

🎯 Vision & Mission

Vision:

To emerge as a premier center of technical education and research in Computer Science, fostering innovative engineers capable of solving global societal and technological challenges.

Mission:

  • Deliver contemporary curriculum through experiential learning and project-based pedagogies.
  • Establish state-of-the-art laboratory facilities fostering research in AI, Cloud, and Cybersecurity.
  • Cultivate industry partnerships, hackathons, and internship pathways for seamless student placement.
  • Instill professional ethics, leadership qualities, and a mindset for lifelong learning.

📚 Academic Programs Offered

  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA) — 2 Years Postgraduate degree focused on Enterprise Software, Web Architecture, and Cloud Systems.
  • B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering — 4 Years Undergraduate program covering full-stack development, algorithms, and systems engineering.
  • M.Tech in Computer Science — 2 Years Advanced specialization in AI, Machine Learning, and Distributed Computing.
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science — Rigorous doctoral research in emerging domains of computing.

🔬 Laboratory Facilities

Our department hosts specialized computing laboratories equipped with modern hardware, high-speed campus networks, and licensed developer suites:

  • Internet & Web Technologies Lab (IWT Lab): Dedicated to full-stack web engineering, Node.js, databases, and modern web frameworks.
  • Advanced Programming & Algorithms Lab: High-performance workstations for C++, Java, and Python algorithmic computing.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Lab: GPU-enabled clusters for deep learning and big data analytics.
  • Database Management Systems (DBMS) Lab: Relational and NoSQL database management and optimization suites.
  • IoT & Embedded Systems Lab: Microcontrollers, sensors, and edge computing kits for smart system design.
